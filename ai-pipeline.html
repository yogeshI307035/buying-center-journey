3Today vs. Phase 2 — adding Opportunity to the loop

Today, the pipeline is grounded in interactions that are already captured against the Account. In Phase 2, the Opportunity becomes a first-class input and output — the buying center lives per-deal, not just per-account, and opportunity attributes also drive party determination directly.

MVP — what ships first

  • Scope: Buying Center on the Account Detail page, 4 roles (DM · Champion · Influencer · Blocker) + "Influences" edge type.
  • Inputs: Email plainContent, Appointment notes[], Visit notes[], 90-day window.
  • Output: badged role with confidence + 2–3 evidence snippets; dashed edges on a relationship graph.
  • Rep control: accept / edit / reject every suggestion. Manual overrides are logged and excluded from re-inference on that pair.
  • Data residency: prompt + context stays within SAP AI Core on the customer's BTP tenant — no third-party egress.

Phase 2 — Opportunity in the loop

  • New input — Opportunity context: header (size band, LoB, country, stage, strategic flag), partyRoles already assigned by the rep, notes[] / hyperNotes[].
  • Per-deal buying center: same contact can be a Champion on one deal and an Influencer on another — role is deal-scoped, not just account-scoped.
  • Cross-signal with Party Determination: opportunity attributes already drive From Business Rules party determination for Owner / Sales Employee / Sales Unit. AI suggests rule rows from historical outcomes; admin activates. Rules still run at determination time.
  • Relationship subtypes: expand from "Influences" to Reports To, Advocates For, Opposes, Works With, Mentors.
  • Warmest-path surfacing: "Your closest confirmed path to the DM on this opportunity is You → Sabine → Katrin."
  • Additional sources: Phone Call transcripts, LinkedIn (if Sales Navigator is integrated) — same prompt shape, richer signal.

4Guardrails — what the AI will and will not do

Will

  • Suggest roles and influence edges, always with confidence + evidence.
  • Stay within the rep's own authorization scope — only contacts the rep can already see.
  • Log every accept / edit / reject for model improvement.
  • Return "Unknown" when interaction history is thin, rather than guessing.

Will not

  • Overwrite rep-confirmed roles or edges silently — dashed edges never auto-promote to solid.
  • Grant visibility to contacts the rep is not authorized to see.
  • Override the Party Determination scheme or change DetMeth on successful rule-based determinations.
  • Send prompt payloads outside the SAP AI Core tenant boundary.
