1. The deal, in one paragraph

Weiland Bau GmbH — a 70-year-old German construction firm — runs an aging, multi-brand cordless tool fleet across 14 sites. The new COO wants a single-vendor ecosystem, inventory tracking, and a 3-year framework. Bosch Power Tools is competing against two incumbents. Rep Lena Müller has been working the opportunity since September. Today is Nov 12, 2026. Proposal is in, board decision is Dec 10.

Visit 12026-09-30 · Week 4 · 60 minStuttgart HQ — first meeting with Katrin
Katrin Weiland (COO), Sabine Hoffmann, Lena Müller · Author: Lena Müller
First face to face with Katrin. Sharp, direct, no small talk. Her office, 45 min scheduled, ran 60. Katrin opened by saying she has "approximately zero interest in which brand of drill the crews use" — her concern is TCO, utilization data, and whether Weiland can prove 15% cost reduction on tooling spend to the board by FY28. Repeated the 15% number three times. That is the number. She asked: - "What is the total cost of ownership over 5 years including batteries?" - "What's the risk if TrackMyTools has an outage during a project?" - "Who else of your size has committed to this consignment model?" - "If we sign a 3-year framework and the technology changes, where does that leave us?" She did NOT ask about product specs. Not once. She mentioned the board decision is scheduled for mid-December and that she needs the full business case landed with the CFO by end of November. Said "I will make the call on this, but I will not make it without Markus's commercial sign-off." Sabine was quiet during the meeting — deliberately, I think. Let Katrin drive. On the walk back to the lobby Sabine said "that was about as positive as Katrin gets, she liked you." Next step: I prepare the full business case (TCO + risk mitigation section) and send by Oct 7. Katrin to review before the commercial meeting with Markus in November. One flag: Thomas is in Region South and was NOT mentioned once by Katrin. I don't think he's in her inner circle. But he has the Munich veto on the pilot sites. Sabine confirmed afterwards: "Thomas can't kill the deal but he can slow the pilot by 6 months, which is the same thing."
Role signals in this note
  • Katrin (DM, textbook): five independent DM language/behavioral signals in one note. Confidence should be High.
  • Markus (DM commercial scope): "I will not make it without Markus's commercial sign-off".
  • Sabine (Champion): reads the room, flags Thomas risk, interprets COO's mood for the rep.
  • Thomas (Blocker, reinforced): named as slowing the pilot — no direct quote from him here, but the Champion's description of his behavior is a strong indirect signal.

Visit 22026-10-21 · Week 7 · 3hMunich site demo — things go sideways
Thomas Keller + 4 foremen + 2 crew leads · Lena Müller + Bosch demo tech · Author: Lena Müller
Mixed day. Not great. Workshop part went fine. Crews liked the 18V ProCORE batteries, feedback on the GSB 18V-150 drill was positive, foremen made jokes about swapping their current batteries for Bosch ones. Good engagement. Jobsite part — rough. We'd set up TrackMyTools at the Garching site for the demo. One of the reader hubs lost power during the walkthrough and for about 20 min none of the tagged tools showed up on the live dashboard. Thomas pointed at this twice in front of his foremen and said "and this is what you want us to rely on across 14 sites?" The battery exchange program with the existing competitor stock also came up and Thomas challenged me publicly — "we spent €180K on [competitor] batteries in 2024 alone, are you suggesting we throw that away?" I had an answer but it clearly didn't land. He walked me back to the car afterward. His tone was polite but he said, twice, "I'm not sure this is the right time for us to switch — maybe start with one region, not Region South." That's a direct quote. My read: he started neutral in September, now he's actively looking for reasons to delay. Battery sunk cost is real; the hub outage during the demo was a gift to his argument. I need Sabine to help here. Also need to brief Katrin before she hears this from Thomas's own chain. Follow-up: - Debrief with Sabine on Oct 23 - Send Thomas the battery transition buyback proposal (we have one, I didn't lead with it) by Oct 24 - Pre-brief Katrin verbally — not in writing — next time I'm in Stuttgart
Role signals in this note
  • Thomas (Blocker, full commitment): three independent Blocker language/behavioral signals — public objection, ROI dispute, "I'm not sure this is the right time". This is where the drift completes.
  • Signal-weighting test: if the AI reads only Appointment 1 (Thomas sounds cautious), Thomas = Blocker Low or Neutral. With Visit 2 added, Thomas = Blocker Medium–High. Dev team can toggle this record on/off to verify recency weighting.
Email 42026-10-28 · Week 8Thomas → Sabine, cc Lena — formal pushback
From Thomas Keller · To Sabine Hoffmann · cc Lena Müller
Sabine, Writing to you directly because I think we're moving too fast on this. I've got serious concerns about the Munich demo. Three things I need before Region South signs up for any pilot: 1. A written explanation of what happened with the hub outage and what the recovery process is when this happens on an active site. 2. A full cost accounting of what we do with the €180K of existing battery stock. "Buyback" is fine as a word, I want numbers. 3. A phased approach — not Region South first. Role signals in this email

6. How to test using this journey

  1. Seed the tenant. Create Weiland Bau GmbH as an account. Create the 7 contacts with the titles and departments shown above. Open the opportunity "Weiland — Fleet Modernization" with close date 2026-12-15 and value €1.8M.
  2. Load the interactions. For each artifact, create an Email / Appointment / Visit record in Sales Cloud V2 with the plainContent or notes[] shown, dated per the timeline. Link each to the Weiland account and the right contacts.
  3. Run role determination. Trigger the role suggestion job for the Weiland Buying Center.
  4. Compare against section 5. The AI output should match within one role shift on Markus. Evidence snippets must come from the right artifacts.
  5. Test the edge cases:
    • Remove all of Thomas's interactions. Re-run. Expected: Thomas → Unknown Low.
    • Remove only Visit 2 and Email 4. Re-run. Expected: Thomas shifts from Blocker to Influencer or Neutral — this tests recent-signal weighting.
    • Add a second 11-word email from Peter. Re-run. Expected: still Unknown, still Low.
